# LUME — tray assembly views

The ivory plate is the whole tray. The sage tongue is part of the corner holder; it is not a separate clip. The exploded view shows only a cropped tray corner to expose the matching hole.

1. Fit the four holders onto the posts, seated on the upper rail sleeves.
2. Orient the tray so its two catch apertures align with the diagonal retaining tongues.
3. Lower the tray onto all four reinforced seats and press down until the two retaining hooks engage above the tray.
4. To remove it, press the two diagonal tongues toward the centre and lift the tray.

The four seats carry the downward load. Two diagonal hooks resist lift-out. The other two tray apertures provide tongue clearance for easier two-handed release. The holder tongues and the tray are actual exported CAD geometry. Illustration colours identify parts and do not prescribe production colours.

These renders show the mechanical prototype. Physical retention strength, click sound, snap force, creep and heat performance are not established by an image or collision test. Refer to the revision's CAD and slicer reports for the checks actually performed.
