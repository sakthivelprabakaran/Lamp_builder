# Four-post lamp prototypes

Lume includes twelve table studies and three standing studies. Posts are purchased 25 mm square; wood species and actual measured section are unconfirmed. The possible double-rod version is recorded for later, with no assumed construction.

Standing dimensions are provisional. `exposed_leg_height` and `illuminated_height` are separate parameters; the latter starts at 304.8 mm. Pearl starts with 1066.8 mm legs + 304.8 mm light (1371.6 mm overall). Reed shows 1219.2 + 304.8 mm (1524 mm overall). Folio shows the earlier total-height interpretation: 914.4 + 304.8 mm (1219.2 mm overall). The customer slider displays total height, and the caption reports leg/light heights.

All three standing variants use clean exposed posts and four individual printed foot caps. The variants differ in the upper panel pattern and provisional leg height. Two horizontal rail frames with selected PETG dovetail joints are now included; no diagonal braces, shelf or outriggers. See the updated ASSEMBLY_PLAN.md.

Printable parts include flat patterned panel segments, bottom shelf collars, through collars, four top retaining caps, a rod-fit coupon, removable cable guide/cover lengths, strain-relief plate, joining plate and light tray. Panels print flat, collars with bore vertical, all parts in their exported orientation. Every export validates closed, consistently wound meshes and the A1 part envelope/overhang screen. The assembled lamp exceeds the printer envelope intentionally.

Default total rod clearance is 0.6 mm (0.3 mm per side); default panel slot clearance is 0.4 mm total. Slots provide 8.8 mm nominal engagement. Print the coupon against measured posts before batching collars. Bottom collars support the tray and panel stack; through collars receive panel seams. Top retaining caps use accessible owner-selected fasteners into post ends. Removal of these caps and upper collars releases the panels. No adhesive or hidden captive screw is required.

The low-voltage cable follows an accessible open tray edge entry, then an external U channel along a solid post. Guide/cover lengths are at most 220 mm and quantities scale with height. The removable cover and strain-relief plate use ties; cable bend radius and pull resistance need physical checks. Light hardware is a qualitative preview. A specific LED, diffuser and mount have not been selected.

Wood grooves are undecided. The current prototype uses reversible printed receivers around the posts. Section and clearance checks are documented in ASSEMBLY_VALIDATION.md; mesh integrity alone does not verify assembly.

Before use: measure post size; print fit coupon; assemble one upper module; test panel retention and collar sliding; test racking, tipping in all directions, fastener pull-out and repeated panel replacement; test cable pull and bend radius; verify chosen light hardware and operating temperature. Physical fit, loads, stability and thermal/electrical suitability remain unverified. Material has not been decided. Bambu review uses provisional Generic PLA and supports off.
